BrewTube

Wii Homebrew app to watch YouTube videos on



WARNING: THIS IS STILL IN EARLY DEVELOPMENT

About the Project

With BrewTube you can watch YouTube videos on the Wii, using the Homebrew channel.

How to setup

    Requirements

  • • A computer with either a USB or an SD card.
  • • A modded Wii (with Homebrew) Mod your Wii here

  • • 1. Download the latest version of BrewTube from the GitHub
  • • 2. Extract and move the apps folder to the root of your USB or SD card.
  • • 3. Plug in your SD card or USB to the Wii and launch the Homebrew channel.
  • • 4. Launch the BrewTube app.
